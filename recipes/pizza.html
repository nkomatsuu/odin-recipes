Pizza


Deskripsi

Make this homemade pizza with basic ingredients, then top it with any of your favorite toppings.

Bahan-bahan

  • 250g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ½ tsp sal
  • 150 ml warm water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp tomato sauce or bolognese sauce
  • 100g mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 50g sausage or pepperoni, sliced
  • ½ onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Langkah-langkah

  1. Activate the yeast: In a small bowl, mix warm water, sugar, and instant yeast. Stir well and let it sit for 5–10 minutes until it becomes frothy and bubbly.
  2. Make the dough: In a large bowl, combine the flour and salt. Pour in the yeast mixture and olive oil. Mix well until it forms a rough dough.
  3. Knead the dough: Turn the dough out onto a clean surface and knead it with your hands for about 10 minutes until it becomes smooth and elastic.
  4. Let it rise: Place the dough into a lightly oiled bowl, cover it with a clean cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for 30–45 minutes (or until it doubles in size).
  5. Shape the dough: Punch down the risen dough, then roll or stretch it out into a flat circular shape (about 0.3 cm to 1 cm thick) on a floured surface or a baking tray. Prick the center lightly with a fork.
  6. Bake: Preheat your oven to 220°C (425°F). Bake the pizza for 10–15 minutes until the crust turns golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
